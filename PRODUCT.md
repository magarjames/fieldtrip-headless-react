# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

This concept storefront serves people buying practical, design-led clothing for commuting, travel, and late city movement. They need to understand the collection quickly, inspect an item, choose a size and color, and add it to a bag from a phone or desktop.

## Product Purpose

Northline is an inferred concept brand for a dropshipping clothing store. This first surface demonstrates how a fashion-led store can present curated outerwear, bottoms, and carry goods before real supplier data is connected.

## Positioning

The collection is framed around utility layers that solve ordinary city-weather and carry needs without performance claims that cannot yet be verified.

## Operating Context

The website is a TanStack Start and React application. The existing Count Club and Editions routes are separate products and must remain intact. The new storefront lives at \`/northline\`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Product names, descriptions, prices, inventory, and imagery on this route are sample content for a concept storefront.
- A real supplier feed, checkout provider, fulfillment rules, taxes, and stock availability remain open decisions.
- The route provides browse, filter, quick-view, bag, and newsletter-feedback interactions without pretending to process a real order.

## Brand Commitments

- Brand name: Northline.
- The user referenced Shopify Editions as an inspiration for a high-contrast editorial ecommerce experience, not as content to copy.
- Original generated campaign imagery is available in \`src/assets/northline/\`.

## Evidence on Hand

- The user asked for a clothing dropshipping storefront and an upfront website.
- The project includes React, TanStack Router, react-three-fiber, Three.js, and ShaderGradient.
- There is no confirmed supplier catalog, inventory feed, price list, checkout integration, or existing Northline logo.

## Product Principles

- Make the collection legible before making it elaborate.
- Use motion to show hierarchy, product state, and material, not decoration.
- Preserve a clear path from product discovery to bag interaction.
- Do not fabricate commercial proof, availability, or fulfillment claims.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Keep readable contrast, visible keyboard focus, descriptive imagery, semantic headings, and reduced-motion fallbacks.
